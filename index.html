About Claudienne

20 years. $500M+ in personal sales.

Claudienne Hibbert-Smith has spent over two decades in real estate, personally closing more than $500 million in transactions. She founded TRU Real Estate Exchange and the Hibbert Group, and operates in partnership with eXp Realty in Miami. She is the founder of NAREW, the National Association of Real Estate Women.

Her audience is licensed agents at every stage: new agents looking for a first 10 deals, seasoned producers stuck at $5M and trying to break $20M, and team leaders ready to scale to seven figures in personal production. The same plays she still runs today, taught directly inside the community and 1-on-1.

Featured in The New York Finance, Wealth Millionaires, and Black Pages Miami. Currently developing the Everything Real Estate television series with the goal of creating 1,000 new homeowners alongside a training program for young realtors.
